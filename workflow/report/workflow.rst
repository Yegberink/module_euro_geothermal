Geothermal potential
====================

This report collects the geothermal workflow's applicable figures and summary
tables, together with Snakemake's workflow and execution metadata.

Electricity diagnostics describe the available electricity resource before
regional price filtering and heat reservation. In combined heat-and-power mode,
the regional overview instead shows electricity capacity remaining after heat
allocation. Land eligibility, when enabled, is already applied to the resource.

LCOE and cumulative figures require LCOE minimization. The regional map also
requires cutoff aggregation. Depth and cost diagnostics and the study-wide
summary are available for both electricity aggregation methods.
